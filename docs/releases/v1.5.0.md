## AI assistant

PortDock now has a built-in chat assistant, powered by any model available on OpenRouter.

### What's new

- **Assistant in the bottom-right corner.** Ask about deployments, domains, Docker or PortDock itself. Replies appear as they are generated, with formatted code blocks, copy buttons and a Stop button. Minimize it to a button or expand it to a larger window.
- **Conversation history for every user.** Conversations are saved on your PortDock instance and are visible only to the person who started them. Reopen or delete them from the history list.
- **Settings → AI assistant** for administrators:
  - add your OpenRouter API key (stored encrypted);
  - pick a model from the full OpenRouter catalog (search, context size, price per 1M tokens, free-models filter) or type any model id, for example `anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.5`;
  - check the key and the model with **Test connection**;
  - choose who can use the assistant: all users, administrators only, or selected users;
  - add optional instructions to its system prompt, such as team conventions or tone.
- The assistant answers in the user's interface language: English, Romanian or Russian.

### Good to know

- The assistant stays off until an administrator enables it.
- Messages are sent to OpenRouter and to the provider of the selected model, so don't paste secrets into the chat. The assistant only sees what is written in the chat; it has no access to your servers or data.
- The new `PORTDOCK_OPENROUTER_URL` environment variable points the assistant at an OpenRouter-compatible gateway, for networks without direct internet access.

### Upgrading

Update from the sidebar (**Update available**) or from **Settings → Updates**. Nothing needs to be migrated: the new tables are created when PortDock starts.
