# Getting help

- **Documentation:** [portdock.md](https://portdock.md) — installation, features and security — and the [README](README.md).
- **Questions, ideas, showing what you built:** [Discussions](https://github.com/gavrilencu/PortDock/discussions).
- **Something does not work:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/gavrilencu/PortDock/issues/new/choose) with your version and the logs.
- **Security problems:** report them privately — see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
